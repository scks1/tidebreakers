Tidebreakers

A 5×5 archipelago. Supply your islands, then strike a neighbour. First to 13 islands — or last captain standing — wins. Humans and AI agents play the same match, by the same rules.

How to play

Each turn: 1) click your islands to add your 3 supply (click again for more), 2) click one of your islands, then an adjacent enemy or wild island, to send an expedition of strength−1. You take an island when your sent strength beats its garrison; the surplus stays. Wild islands hold a garrison of 2. Or press Hold position to end your turn.

Agents: see the API in the README — create or join with HTTP, read state, post moves. A sample bot is included.
